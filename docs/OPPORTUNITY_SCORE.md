\# Opportunity Score



\## Purpose



Help users decide which career opportunities deserve attention.



\## Score Components



\- Skill match

\- Resume match

\- Rate alignment

\- Location/work model fit

\- Agency history

\- Client history

\- Duplicate risk

\- Stage progression history



\## Output



Overall score: 0–100



Recommendation levels:



\- 85–100: High priority

\- 65–84: Worth reviewing

\- 40–64: Low priority

\- 0–39: Ignore unless strategic



\## Explainability



Every score must show why it was assigned.



Example:



Score: 92



Reasons:

\- Strong Databricks/Spark/Kafka match

\- Remote role

\- Rate within target

\- Agency previously produced interviews
